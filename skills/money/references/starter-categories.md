# Starter budget categories

A generic starting list. Offer it by group, keep what the user recognizes, drop the rest, and add their own. Use the user's own words for a category when they offer one.

"Carries over" marks categories where money left at the end of a month is usually meant to build up for a later bill. It is only a starting suggestion; ask.

Two names are reserved and are not budget categories:

- **Transfer**: money moved between the user's own accounts, and payments to their own credit cards. These are ignored, so nothing is counted twice.
- **Uncategorized**: anything not yet sorted. It shows on the budget page as "Needs sorting."

Bills that come a few times a year (insurance, property tax) get their amount in the months they fall due and zero in the others.
