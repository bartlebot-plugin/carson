# How to use Carson

Carson keeps your paperwork in order. You give Carson a document; Carson reads it, files it in the right folder, writes down what matters, and tells you if something needs doing.

## Giving Carson a document

Pick whichever is easiest:

- **In the conversation.** Attach the file or a photo of the paper and say "file this."
- **In the inbox folder.** Put the file in the folder named `_inbox` inside your Carson folder, then say "check my inbox."
{DROPBOX_LINE}

A scan is easier for Carson to read than a plain photo. If your phone offers "Scan document," use that.

## Things you can say

- "File this."
- "Check my inbox."
- "Where is my car insurance policy?"
- "When does my passport expire?"
- "What did the letter from the water company say?"
- "Show me my desk." (A page with today's appointments, your todo list, and my suggestions.)
- "Brief me." (What is on today and what is due.)
- "How did today go?" (What got done and what is left.)
- "What is waiting for me?" (Carson lists its suggestions you have not answered yet.)
- "Tidy up." (Carson checks that everything is filed properly.)
- "Set up my budget." (Carson keeps a simple budget from your statements.)
- "Show me my budget." (What you planned, what you spent, and what is left.)
- "Learn from this." (Carson makes a correction into a standing rule.)
- "Set up Carson." (To change any of your choices.)

If Carson gets something wrong, just say so: "Vet bills go under Pets." Carson will say "Noted" and remember.

## What Carson will not do

- Carson never deletes anything.
- Carson never sends email or messages to anyone.
- Carson never pays a bill or moves money.
- Carson asks before adding anything to your todo list or calendar{ASK_FIRST_NOTE}.

## Please remember

- Carson is not a financial, tax, legal, or medical adviser. It organizes and summarizes.
- Carson can make mistakes. Check dates, amounts, and deadlines against the paper before you rely on them.
- Your documents are read by Claude, the AI service you are signed in to, under your own Claude account. The person who wrote Carson never receives anything.
- You are responsible for keeping your computer, your accounts, and this folder safe, and for keeping a backup.
