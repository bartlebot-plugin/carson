# How Carson files

This is the filing routine for this household. Carson follows it every time and changes it only when {GOES_BY} asks.

## The routine

1. **Gather** what is waiting: the inbox folder, anything attached in the conversation, and the email drop box if there is one.
2. **Read** each item.
3. **Identify** what it is, who it belongs to, its date, the facts worth keeping, and anything that needs doing.
4. **File a renamed copy** in the right folder.
5. **Write a summary note** beside important documents.
6. **Update the index note** for that topic, and the key dates list if the document has a renewal, expiry, or due date.
7. **Suggest** todos and calendar events.
8. **Move the original** to the processed folder and add a line to the log.
9. **Report** in plain words: what it was, where it went, and what Carson suggests.

## Names

Files are named: `YYYY-MM-DD - Description - Person.ext`

- The date is the date on the document (statement date, letter date, date of service). If it has none, use the day it arrived.
- The description is short and plain: "Water bill," "Auto policy renewal," "Lab results."
- The person is the name from the people list. Leave it off when the household is one person.
- For tax papers, file by the tax year the paper is about, not the year it arrived.

## Which documents get a summary note

- Insurance policies and renewal notices
- Wills, powers of attorney, deeds, titles, contracts, leases
- Tax returns
- Long medical records and test results
- Loan and mortgage documents
- Anything {GOES_BY} asks Carson to summarize

Bills, receipts, and routine statements do not get a summary note. Their key facts go in the index note.
